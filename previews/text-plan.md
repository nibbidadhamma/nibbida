## 1. Start page

**Top of the page (kept):** the Leonard Cohen quote.

**Letter** (replaces the current letter; headings kept from the old site)

> ### The way in is the way out…
>
> The Internet can feel like a maze, built to hold our attention and make us forget where we were going. *Nibbidā* is meant to do the opposite. The word means disenchantment, a quiet turning away from what does not lead to freedom. Here nothing is sold, and nothing is asking for your attention.
>
> ### …and the way out is through.
>
> Nibbidā is a small, independent Dhamma publisher. It prepares books and resources for people who want to understand their own minds more deeply, and who, like the Buddha, take it that there is a way out of the labyrinth for those who walk through it with growing clarity. Meditation is the core of that work. Around it there is much that helps: the texts, the languages, the places to practice.
>
> I hope the work here is useful, and that some of you will want to take part in it. May it make your own study and practice a little easier.
>
> *Jayadhamma Bhikkhu*
