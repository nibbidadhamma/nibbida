Ānandajoti Bhikkhu — Texts from the Canon

Ānandajoti Bhikkhu

Eight translations and editions of canonical texts by Ānandajoti Bhikkhu, the larger works first. The files are in the Dhamma-Dāna Drive archive. The author's website is ancient-buddhist-texts.net.

The books of this collection are listed below. Each button opens the file in the Dhamma-Dāna Drive archive.

  1. Jātaka, The Birth Stories

    547 stories from the Jātaka Commentary. Translation edited by E. B. Cowell, revised and reconstructed by Ānandajoti Bhikkhu (EPUB dated 2021-11-17).

    Open EPUB

  2. The Earliest Recorded Discourses of the Buddha

    From the Lalitavistara, the Mahākhandaka and the Mahāvastu. Simplified translation (EPUB dated 2020-01-27).

    Open EPUB

  3. Dhammapada, Dhamma Verses

    The Dhamma Verses (Khuddaka Nikāya 2). The author's website lists it in Pāli and English (EPUB dated 2017-08-02).

    Open EPUB

  4. Patna Dhammapada

    The Patna Dhamma Verses, text transcribed by Margaret Cone (1989), translated by Ānandajoti Bhikkhu (2017).

    No licence is stated in the e-book. It says: “Dr. Cone’s text has been reproduced with the kind consent of The Pali Text Society, Oxford, England which retains the copyright”.

    Open EPUB

  5. Udāna, Exalted Utterances

    Khuddaka Nikāya 3. The author's website lists it in Pāli and English (EPUB dated 2018-12-02).

    Open EPUB

  6. Khuddakapāṭha, The Short Readings

    Khuddaka Nikāya 1. The author's website lists it in Pāli and English (EPUB dated 2020-01-29).

    Open EPUB

  7. The Stories about the Foremost Elder Nuns

    From the Aṅguttara Nikāya and its commentary, 8.51–53 (EPUB dated 2019-02-27).

    Open EPUB

  8. Bhikkhu Pātimokkha (BJT)

    The e-book's own description: “Readings for the rules taken from BJT Parajikapali & Pacittiyapali”. Edited by Ānandajoti Bhikkhu (EPUB dated 2018-04-01).

    Open EPUB
