Meditation Map of the Dhamma

Nice places around the world for practicing Buddhist meditation

With the Meditation Map of the Dhamma it is now even easier to share gems and find new places for exploring the Buddha’s theory and practice.

In the spirit of one Dhamma/Dharma, places from different traditions are included here. Some of the places give you an opportunity to practice meditation for an extensive period of time, perhaps even for years, on a donation basis. This is truly an amazing gift for those who want to go deeper in the Dhamma. It is also generosity and dāna-culture at its best. Please allow yourself to become a part of this generous spirit when you enter these communities, and try to find a way to give something back. Remember the preciousness of your stay, for yourself and for future meditators.

Share the map as you like. If you want to contribute a new place, give an update, or even write a review of a place on the list, please write to me.

May your practice be fruitful, wherever you go.

Places

The map is drawn with OpenStreetMap and CARTO. It loads only when you press the button.

  • Ajahn ChahAmaravati Buddhist MonasteryNear London, EnglandThe biggest and most accessible monastery in the Ajahn Chah tradition. Dāna-based.
  • InsightGaia HouseNear Newton Abbot, Devon, EnglandA silent retreat center founded in 1983, with retreats mainly based on insight (vipassanā), loving-kindness and Zen practice. It also hosts long-term solitary retreatants. Fee charged.
  • InsightIMS Retreat CenterBarre, near Worcester, Massachusetts, USAThe retreat center of the Insight Meditation Society, founded in 1975 and rooted in the Theravāda tradition. It offers residential insight (vipassanā) and loving-kindness (mettā) retreats through the year. The Forest Refuge on the same campus is for longer stays. Fee charged, with teachers supported by voluntary dāna.
  • InsightInsight Meditation CenterRedwood City, near San Francisco, California, USAA community center for insight (vipassanā) meditation, established by Gil Fronsdal, who guides it with Andrea Fella. It offers weekly sittings, talks and classes, and runs retreats at its center in Santa Cruz. Dāna-based.
  • Tibetan (Gelug)Kopan MonasteryNear Kathmandu, NepalA Tibetan Buddhist monastery in the Gelug tradition, affiliated with the FPMT, overlooking the Kathmandu Valley. It offers courses and retreats. Daily fee.
  • GalduwaNa Uyana Forest MonasteryMelsiripura, near Kurunegala, Sri LankaA forest monastery of the Sri Kalyani Yogasrama Samstha, in the Galduwa tradition, covering more than 5,000 acres in the Dummiya mountain range, with about 150 monks. The site offers meditation instruction, Dhamma sermons and an application procedure for foreign meditators. Dāna-based.
  • Pa-AukPa-Auk Tawya Meditation Centre, Pyin Oo LwinPyin Oo Lwin, near Mandalay, MyanmarA branch of the Pa-Auk Tawya Meditation Centre, at Kyauk-phyar-doe village in Pyin Oo Lwin Township. Applications to stay are made through the separate Pa-Auk society website. Dāna-based.
  • MahāsiPanditarama Lumbini Vipassana Meditation CenterLumbini, NepalA center in the Mahāsi tradition, teaching intensive vipassanā in Lumbini, the Buddha's birthplace. It offers sitting and walking meditation, interviews and Dhamma talks in English, with noble silence and eight precepts. Dāna-based.
  • Zen (Vietnamese)Plum VillageThénac, near Bergerac, Dordogne, FranceA monastery in the Vietnamese Zen tradition of Thich Nhat Hanh, where the Plum Village monks live. Guests stay through registered retreats. Fee charged.
  • Other TheravādaShwe Oo Min Meditation CentreMingaladon, near Yangon, MyanmarA forest meditation center on the edge of Yangon, where Sayadaw U Tejaniya is the meditation teacher. The teaching is mindfulness practice based on the Satipaṭṭhāna Sutta. Dāna-based.
  • Tibetan (Gelug)Tushita Meditation CentreMcLeod Ganj, Dharamsala, IndiaA center in the Tibetan Gelug tradition (FPMT), with drop-in meditations, introductory courses and group retreats, in forested hills above McLeod Ganj. Fee charged.
  • Other TheravādaAnenja ViharaRettenberg, near Kempten, Allgäu, GermanyA Theravāda nunnery dedicated to building a bhikkhunī saṅgha in Germany and Europe. Guests are welcome by registration. Dāna-based.
  • Non-sectarianBeatenberg Meditation CenterBeatenberg, above Interlaken and Lake Thun, SwitzerlandA Buddhist meditation center at about 1,250 m, with views of Lake Thun and the Bernese Alps. It offers retreats from three days to four weeks, led by various teachers, with emphasis on vipassanā and the heart practices of mettā and karuṇā, and some Tibetan practices. Fee charged.
  • GalduwaBhaddeka Vihari Aranya Senasanaya - Thanjan Tenna BalangodaTanjan Tenna, southeast of Balangoda, Sri LankaAlso called Thanjan Tenna. A group of forest hermitages that began in 2003, when Ven. Wagadapola Wimalagnana built a hut in the forest near the village, about 23 km southeast of Balangoda. It offers Dhamma talks in Sinhala and English, and the hermitages are open to monks, Sri Lankan and foreign, who wish to stay. Dāna-based.
  • Ajahn ChahBirken Forest Buddhist MonasteryNear Kamloops, British Columbia, CanadaA monastery in the Thai Forest tradition of Ajahn Chah, in a secluded forest near Kamloops. It runs off-grid, supported entirely by donations, and teachings, lodging and meals are free. Dāna-based.
  • ZenBodhi ZendoNear Kodaikanal, Tamil Nadu, IndiaA Zen training center in the Kodaikanal hills, founded by P. Ama Samy. It holds sesshins twice a month, with daily zazen, and visitors apply in advance. Teaching is free; daily fee for lodging and meals.
  • Ajahn ChahBodhinyanarama Buddhist MonasteryStokes Valley, Lower Hutt, near Wellington, New ZealandA monastery in the lineage of Ajahn Chah and Ajahn Sumedho, in a wooded valley. It is mainly a residence for monastic training, with a place for lay visitors. It holds a weekly Sunday meditation and retreats. Dāna-based.
  • Ajahn ChahBodhisaddha Forest MonasteryWilton, near Wollongong and Sydney, AustraliaA monastery in the Thai Forest tradition of Ajahn Chah, about 20 minutes from Wollongong and an hour from Sydney. A community of monks lives there, and visiting hours are listed on the website. Dāna-based.
  • Other TheravādaBoonkanjanaram Meditation CenterPattaya, Chonburi, ThailandA meditation hermitage near Pattaya where visitors practice vipassanā on the four foundations of mindfulness, in the method of Ajahn Naeb. Guests stay in small individual huts. Daily fee.
  • Non-sectarianBuddhist Retreat Centre (BRC Ixopo)Ixopo, inland from Durban, KwaZulu-Natal, South AfricaA retreat center on a country estate, established about forty years ago, which people of all religions visit. It offers scheduled retreats, self-retreats, accommodation and day visits. Fee charged.
  • Other TheravādaBukit Bendera Sitavana ViharaNorthern MalaysiaA monastery. Dāna-based.
  • MahāsiChanmyay Myaing Meditation CentreMingaladon, Yangon, MyanmarA meditation center in the Mahāsi tradition, founded in 1999 by Sayadaw U Indaka, with teaching translated into English. Dāna-based.
  • Ajahn ChahChithurst Buddhist MonasteryNear Petersfield, West Sussex, EnglandCittaviveka, a monastery in the Thai Forest tradition of Ajahn Chah, founded in 1979. It is the oldest monastery of the Ajahn Chah tradition in the West, and day visitors and overnight guests are welcome. Dāna-based.
  • Goenka · Sayagyi U Ba KhinDhamma Dvara Vipassana Meditation CentreTriebel, near Plauen and Hof, Vogtland, GermanyA center in the tradition of Sayagyi U Ba Khin as taught by S.N. Goenka, in the Saxon Vogtland. It holds ten-day courses, usually two a month. Dāna-based.
  • Goenka · Sayagyi U Ba KhinDhamma Mahī Centre VipassanaLouesme, near Auxerre, Burgundy, FranceA center in the tradition of Sayagyi U Ba Khin as taught by S.N. Goenka, in the Yonne department of Burgundy. It holds ten-day courses. Dāna-based.
  • Goenka · Sayagyi U Ba KhinDhamma Medini Vipassana Meditation CentreNear Kaukapakapa, north of Auckland, New ZealandA center in the tradition of Sayagyi U Ba Khin as taught by S.N. Goenka, in the Makarau valley about an hour from Auckland. It holds ten-day residential courses, run on donations. Dāna-based.
  • Ajahn Tong · MahāsiDhammacari Vipassana Meditation CenterSonnenthal, near Rottenburg an der Laaber and Landshut, Bavaria, GermanyA center in the Ajahn Tong tradition, a variation of the Mahāsi noting technique, taught by Hildegard Huber (Dhammacari). It offers 15-day courses for beginners. Dāna-based.
  • Ajahn ChahDhammagiri Forest HermitageKholo, near Brisbane, Queensland, AustraliaA small forest hermitage in the Ajahn Chah tradition, about 45 minutes from Brisbane, led by Ajahn Dhammasīha. It offers weekend gatherings, one-day retreats and a podcast of Dhamma talks. Dāna-based.
  • Ajahn TongDhammanikhom Vipassana Meditation CenterHeinersbrück, near Cottbus, GermanyA Theravāda nunnery (bhikkhunī arama) and meditation center in the lineage of Ajahn Tong. It offers stays at the monastery with meditation guidance. The site is in German and English. Dāna-based.
  • BuddhadāsaDipabhāvan Meditation CenterMaret, Koh Samui, ThailandA meditation center on Koh Samui, founded by Ajahn Poh of Suan Mokkh, in the Buddhadāsa tradition. It holds silent courses of several days. Dāna-based.
  • Tibetan (Kagyu)Dongyu Gatsal Ling NunneryNear Palampur and Dharamsala, Himachal Pradesh, IndiaA nunnery for Himalayan women in the Drukpa Kagyu tradition, founded by Jetsunma Tenzin Palmo, where women can also do self-retreat. Fee charged.
  • Sayagyi U Ba KhinInternational Meditation CentreHeddington, near Calne and Bath, Wiltshire, EnglandA center in the tradition of Sayagyi U Ba Khin and Mother Sayamagyi, at Splatts House in the Wiltshire countryside since the late 1970s. It holds ten-day courses. Dāna-based.
  • Sayagyi U Ba KhinInternational Meditation Centre (IMC), YangonBahan, Yangon, MyanmarThe original International Meditation Centre, founded in Yangon (Rangoon) by Sayagyi U Ba Khin in 1952. The worldwide network follows the tradition of Sayagyi U Ba Khin and Mother Sayamagyi. Dāna-based.
  • Ajahn ChahJhana Grove Meditation Retreat CentreSerpentine, near Perth, Western AustraliaThe retreat center of the Buddhist Society of Western Australia, near Bodhinyana Monastery of Ajahn Brahm. It hosts retreats through the year and has single rooms. Fee charged.
  • Other TheravādaMeethirigala Nissarana Vanaya Forest MonasteryMeetirigala, northeast of Colombo, Sri LankaA forest monastery in the strict forest tradition, founded in 1967, with residential retreats and online sessions. Matara Sri Nanarama Maha Thero was its first chief meditation teacher. Dāna-based.
  • Thai Forest (Ajahn Lee)Metta Forest MonasteryNear Valley Center, north of San Diego, California, USAA monastery in the Thai Forest tradition, founded in 1990 by Ajaan Suwat Suvaco and directed by Thanissaro Bhikkhu. Its main purpose is to let men ordain as monks, and laypeople are welcome for day visits or overnight stays. Dāna-based.
  • Ayya KhemaMetta ViharaNear Buchenberg and Kempten, Allgäu, GermanyA monastery in the tradition of Ayya Khema, founded in 1997 and part of the Buddha-Haus project in the Allgäu. It offers meditation stays for people who want to practice there and support the project. Mostly in German. Fee charged.
  • Ajahn ChahMuttodaya Forest MonasteryNear Stammbach and Hof, Bavaria, GermanyA forest monastery in the Thai Forest tradition, founded in 2008 by Ajahn Cattamalo. It is a residence for monks, novices and aspirants, and the site is in German, English and Thai. Dāna-based.
  • Tibetan (Kagyu)Namobuddha MonasteryNamo Buddha, southeast of Kathmandu, NepalThrangu Tashi Yangtse Monastery, a Kagyu monastery on a hilltop in Kavrepalanchok District, associated with Khenchen Thrangu Rinpoche. It has a monastic college, a school for young monks and a medical clinic.
  • Pa-AukNandaka Vihara Meditation SocietyBukit Mertajam, near George Town, Penang, MalaysiaA monastery in the Pa-Auk tradition, in the Cherok Tokun area. It teaches samatha-vipassanā, beginning with mindfulness of breathing, and offers meditation classes and retreats. Dāna-based.
  • Other TheravādaNillambe Buddhist Meditation CentreNillambe, near Kandy, Sri LankaA meditation center in the hills near Kandy. It grew from a group of friends from Kandy who met for meditation sittings at Peradeniya University in the late 1970s, and is long associated with the teacher Godwin Samararatne. Its site lists residential and online programs. Dāna-based.
  • Goenka · Sayagyi U Ba KhinNordens Vipassana Center - Dhamma SobhanaNear Ödeshög, between Stockholm and Gothenburg, SwedenA residential center in the tradition of Sayagyi U Ba Khin as taught by S.N. Goenka, run by the Swedish Vipassanā Foundation since 2007. It holds ten-day courses, usually in English and Swedish. Dāna-based.
  • Pa-AukPa Auk Tawya Vipassana Dhura HermitageRempang island, Batam, near Singapore, IndonesiaA hermitage in the Pa-Auk tradition, about 8 km from the 4th Barelang bridge. Practice begins with samatha meditation to develop absorption (jhāna), and a meditator may choose any of the forty samatha subjects. Visitors are asked to contact the hermitage before arriving. Dāna-based.
  • Pa-AukPa-Auk Forest MonasteryNear Mawlamyine, Mon State, MyanmarThe main monastery of the Pa-Auk tradition, founded in 1926 about 15 km southeast of Mawlamyine. It teaches samatha and vipassanā, and has a large resident community of monks, nuns and lay practitioners, including foreigners. Applications are made through the monastery's website. Dāna-based.
  • Pa-AukPa-Auk Tawya Vipassana Dhura Hermitage, BogorTamansari, Bogor, near Jakarta, IndonesiaA meditation hermitage in the Pa-Auk tradition. Dāna-based.
  • MahāsiPanditarama Shwe Taung Gone Sasana Meditation CentreBahan, Yangon, MyanmarThe main Panditarama center, founded in 1990 by Sayadaw U Paṇḍitābhivaṃsa in the Mahāsi Sayadaw lineage. Dāna-based.
  • Other TheravādaParamita International Buddhist Meditation CenterKadugannawa, near Kandy, Sri LankaA meditation center on a hilltop on a former tea plantation, set up for international visitors with guidance in English. Teachings are free; room and meals are charged.
  • Non-sectarianPenang Bodhi Heart SanctuaryMount Erskine, George Town, Penang, MalaysiaA non-sectarian Buddhist meditation center run by lay people, opened in 2006. It has a meditation center, prayer hall and library, and groups can hold retreats in its halls. Dāna-based.
  • Other TheravādaRockhill Meditation CenterWegirikanda, near Gampola and Kandy, Sri LankaAn international retreat center and hermitage on a 15-acre mountainside, teaching vipassanā in English and Sinhala. Teachings are free; a minimum of US$20 per day is requested for food and lodging.
  • Non-sectarianRocky Mountain Ecodharma Retreat CenterWard, near Boulder, Colorado, USAA retreat center on 180 acres of river, meadow and woodland, next to National Forest and near the Indian Peaks Wilderness. It hosts meditation retreats and workshops with an emphasis on practice in nature, which it calls Ecodharma. Fee charged.
  • Tibetan (Gelug)Root Institute for Wisdom CultureBodh Gaya, Bihar, IndiaA center in the Gelug tradition (FPMT), founded by Lama Thubten Yeshe and Lama Zopa Rinpoche. It offers courses, retreats and pilgrimage programs. Daily fee.
  • Other TheravādaSamanadipa Forest Buddhist MonasteryNear Trebnje and Ljubljana, SloveniaA small forest monastery established in 2016, following the Buddha's teaching as preserved in the Pali Nikāyas and the Vinaya. Its main purpose is seclusion for monks, and male and female visitors and overnight guests are welcome. Dāna-based.
  • Tibetan (Kagyu)Samye Ling Tibetan Buddhist CentreEskdalemuir, near Langholm, ScotlandKagyu Samye Ling Monastery and Tibetan Centre, founded in 1967 and described as the first Tibetan Buddhist center in the West. It offers courses, retreats and guest accommodation, and welcomes day visitors. Fee charged.
  • Other TheravādaSāsanārakkha Buddhist SanctuaryNear Taiping, Perak, MalaysiaA Theravāda monastery and monk training center, established in 2000, with a retreat center for meditation retreats and workshops. Meals and lodging for retreatants are free. Dāna-based.
  • MahāsiSatipanya Buddhist RetreatNear Shrewsbury, Shropshire, EnglandA retreat center in the Mahāsi Sayadaw tradition, founded by Bhante Bodhidhamma. It offers insight meditation retreats, in person and online. The center says it depends on an economy of dāna. Dāna-based.
  • Ajahn ChahSkiptvet Buddhist MonasteryNear Skiptvet, an hour south of Oslo, NorwayA forest monastery in the Ajahn Chah tradition (Lokuttara Vihara), on an old farm with fields and forest. Visitors are welcome during the day and may join the daily routine; overnight stays are booked through a form. Dāna-based.
  • InsightSpirit Rock Meditation CenterWoodacre, Marin County, near San Francisco, California, USAA center in the Insight Meditation tradition, in the hills of West Marin. It offers residential and online retreats from one day to a month or more, classes and drop-in groups, with Jack Kornfield among its teachers. Fee charged, with scholarships.
  • GalduwaSpringhill Forest MonasteryHantana, Kandy, Sri LankaA branch of the Sri Kalyani Yogasrama (Galduwa, Ambalangoda), in the forest reserve of the Hantana range above Kandy, with views of the town. It holds a sīla program on every poya day, and retreats can be requested depending on availability. Dāna-based.
  • BuddhadāsaSuan Mokkh International Dharma HermitageNear Chaiya, Surat Thani, southern ThailandThe English-language retreat center of Wat Suan Mokkh, founded in 1986 in the tradition of Buddhadāsa Bhikkhu.   • Non-sectarianThe Barn Retreat CentreNear Totnes, Devon, EnglandA retreat center on the Sharpham Estate, run by the Sharpham Trust. Up to eleven people live together in single rooms for weekly retreats, with daily meditation and mindful work in the gardens. Fee charged.

It holds regular sittings during the week, offers short stays of one to three days for first-time visitors, and longer residencies and retreats. Fee charged for retreats.
