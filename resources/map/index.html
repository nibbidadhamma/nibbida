Meditation Map of the Dhamma

Nice places around the world for practicing Buddhist meditation

With the Meditation Map of the Dhamma it is now even easier to share gems and find new places for exploring the Buddha’s theory and practice.

In the spirit of one Dhamma/Dharma, places from different traditions are included here. Some of the places give you an opportunity to practice meditation for an extensive period of time, perhaps even for years, on a donation basis. This is truly an amazing gift for those who want to go deeper in the Dhamma. It is also generosity and dāna-culture at its best. Please allow yourself to become a part of this generous spirit when you enter these communities, and try to find a way to give something back. Remember the preciousness of your stay, for yourself and for future meditators.

Share the map as you like. If you want to contribute a new place, give an update, or even write a review of a place on the list, please write to me.

May your practice be fruitful, wherever you go.
