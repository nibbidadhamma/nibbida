About

There is a crack in everything,
that's how the light gets in.

– Leonard Cohen

The way in is the way out...

The Internet can sometimes feel like a giant maze, with websites and apps doing everything they can to suck us in and make us forget our path. I'm happy to tell you that Nibbidā has the exact opposite purpose. It's all about disenchantment here, and no money is involved. If you can find any useful information here in minutes or less, I've done my job correctly.

...and the way out is through.

This site is for "insiders". For people who want to delve deep into their own minds. People who, like the Buddha, have seen that there is a way out of the labyrinth, if we allow ourselves to walk through it with increasing clarity and wisdom. The main method for this progress is, of course, meditation. So that's the kind of cultivation and practice that forms the main focus here. But there's a lot more that's helpful to know about the path to liberation...

I hope you like this initiative, and maybe even want to contribute to one of the projects here. May the site make your own exploration of meditation and Dhamma easier, perhaps even more fruitful.

Warmly welcome!

/Jayadhamma Bhikkhu

Dhamma projects

A map with lots of places to practice Buddhist meditation.

Meditation Map of the Dhamma

This map will help you find nice meditation centers and monasteries around the world to practice Buddhist meditation. In the spirit of one Dhamma/Dharma, places from many different traditions are included here. With this map made in Google Maps, it's now even more easy to share places with fellow meditators, and find new vistas for exploring the Buddha's ancient path.

Dhamma-Dāna Archive

There's a lot of Buddhist books given away online, so called Dhammadāna. But where to find them, and which one to read? Here's a selection for you with more than 300 books in total, all distributed freely as gifts of Dhamma. The books are either from modern meditation teachers and scholars, or translations of old scriptures. All just one click away to read or download.

Pali Resource Guide

This guide gathers the best tools and resources for learning Pali, the language of the early Buddhist texts. It includes apps, dictionaries, Pali keyboards, books, courses, and more. Everything carefully chosen to make reading, studying, and understanding Pali easier and more enjoyable, whether you’re a beginner or more advanced.

A short guide to the application procedure of the Myanmar Meditation Visa.

How to make a Myanmar Meditation Visa

If you are getting serious about your meditation practice, one of the best countries you can travel to is Myanmar (Burma). With its meditation visa (also called religious visa) you are allowed to stay practically as long as you like in Myanmar, if you decide to extend it. This guide will help you through the process how to make the visa, and in Bangkok especially.
