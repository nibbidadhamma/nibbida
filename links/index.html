Links

Good places to
read and listen

Dhamma sites, publishers, tools and podcasts.

If you're looking for places to meditate, you might want to visit the Meditation Map of the Dhamma (coming soon). If you're looking for books to download easily, you can visit the Dhamma-Dāna Archive. A lot of different Pali related links are also available in the Pali Resource Guide.

Most of the links below come from Online Dhamma Resources, a list compiled by Kusalacāra Bhikkhu for the Early Buddhist Obsidian Vault. Many thanks to him for sharing it.

Start here

New to these sites? Here are eight well-known places to begin, from different kinds of resources: sutta texts, a classic archive, talks, a dictionary, a publisher, a magazine and a forum.

SuttaCentral ↗

suttacentral.net

Main hub for early Buddhist texts with multiple translations, parallels, segmented Pāli, and tools.

Access to Insight (archive) ↗

accesstoinsight.org

Classic collection of Theravāda sutta translations and essays (archived but still widely referenced).

Dhamma Talks (Thanissaro Bhikkhu) ↗

dhammatalks.org

Large collection of sutta translations, books, and essays from the Thai Forest tradition.

Dharma Seed ↗

dharmaseed.org

Dhamma talks from most teachers.

Digital Pāli Dictionary (DPDict) ↗

dpdict.net

Modern, searchable Pāli–English dictionary, useful alongside any Pāli text. Online and offline.

Buddhist Publication Society (BPS) ↗

bps.lk

Sri Lankan publisher offering a vast library of downloadable Theravāda books and booklets.

Tricycle ↗

tricycle.org

A well-known Buddhist magazine.

Dhamma Wheel ↗

dhammawheel.com

Large English-language discussion forum focused on Theravāda Buddhism.

Canonical links

Translations (Pāli Nikāyas & related)

SuttaCentral ↗

suttacentral.net

Main hub for early Buddhist texts with multiple translations, parallels, segmented Pāli, and tools.

The Buddha’s Words ↗

thebuddhaswords.net

Clean HTML versions of many Pāli suttas in English and Pāli, organised by Nikāya.

Dhamma.gift ↗

dhamma.gift

Lightweight reader that aggregates sutta texts from multiple sources; good for quick reading.

Dhamma Talks (Thanissaro Bhikkhu) ↗

dhammatalks.org

Large collection of sutta translations, books, and essays from the Thai Forest tradition.

Ancient Buddhist Texts ↗

ancient-buddhist-texts.net

Pāli and English texts, metrical editions, and study material by Ānandajoti Bhikkhu.

Pa-Auk Tipiṭaka Project ↗

tipitaka.paauksociety.org

Online Pāli Canon from the Pa-Auk tradition, with some supporting material.

SuttaFriends ↗

suttafriends.org

Accessible English translations of Pāli suttas, with some Pāli text and topic-based navigation.

Dharmafarer (Sutta Discovery) ↗

themindingcentre.org

Annotated translations and in-depth study notes by Piya Tan; strong focus on early suttas.

Hillside Hermitage – Suttas ↗

suttas.hillsidehermitage.org

Suttas and talks from Ajahn Nyanamoli and the Hillside Hermitage community, practice-focused.

Colombo Dhamma Friends / “Serene Colombo” ↗

serenecolombo.org

Additional English sutta translations and teachings, often overlapping with SuttaFriends.

Access to Insight (archive) ↗

accesstoinsight.org

Classic collection of Theravāda sutta translations and essays (archived but still widely referenced).

ReadingFaithfully.org ↗

readingfaithfully.org

Resources to build a regular sutta reading practice: guides, reading plans, and study tips.

Buddha Dust ↗

buddhadust.net

Extensive index pointing to sutta translations across several websites and collections.

A Buddhist View ↗

abuddhistview.com

Essays and reflections grounded in early Buddhist teachings and practice.

Pāli text & study tools

BuddhaNexus – Pāli ↗

buddhanexus.net

Pāli text viewer with powerful parallel search across Pāli Nikāyas and Chinese Āgamas.

Tipitaka.org ↗

tipitaka.org

Online Pāli Canon in multiple scripts, with line-by-line text navigation.

Digital Pāli Reader (web) ↗

digitalpalireader.online

Browser-based version of the classic DPR: instant word lookup and dictionary integration.

Tipitaka Pali Reader ↗

americanmonk.org

Reader app with integrated dictionaries and search, designed for Pāli Canon study.

Tipitaka.app ↗

tipitaka.app

Mobile-friendly web app for browsing the Pāli Canon.

Tipitaka Sutta (sutta.org) ↗

tipitaka.sutta.org

Alternative front-end to Pāli texts with its own navigation structure.

Digital Pāli Dictionary (DPDict) ↗

dpdict.net

Modern, searchable Pāli–English dictionary, useful alongside any Pāli text. Online and offline.

PTS Converter (Dhamma-Dāna) ↗

pts.dhamma-dana.de

Converts between PTS page references and standard sutta references for the main Nikāyas.

Open.tipitaka.lk ↗

open.tipitaka.lk

Sri Lankan Tipitaka in various scripts and include audio for all pali texts.

Āgamas & other early canons

Dharma Pearls ↗

canon.dharmapearls.net

High-quality English translations of Chinese Āgama collections and related early Buddhist texts.

BDK America (Bukkyō Dendō Kyōkai) ↗

bdkamerica.org

Translations from the Chinese canon, including some Āgama and early Mahāyāna texts.

Dharmamitra ↗

dharmamitra.org

Extensive collection of English translations from the Chinese Buddhist Canon by Bhikshu Dharmamitra, covering Mahāyāna Sūtras, Vinaya, and Śāstras.

Bhikkhu Anālayo – meditation instructions ↗

agamaresearch.dila.edu.tw

A page from the Āgama Research Group (Dharma Drum Institute) that provides meditation‑instruction audio and transcripts by Bhikkhu Anālayo, covering practices like Satipaṭṭhāna (mindfulness), Ānāpānasati (breath mindfulness), the Brahmavihāras, and emptiness.
