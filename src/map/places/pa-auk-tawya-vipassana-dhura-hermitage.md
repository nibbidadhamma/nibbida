A hermitage in the Pa-Auk tradition, about 8 km from the 4th Barelang bridge. Practice begins with samatha meditation to develop absorption (jhāna), and a meditator may choose any of the forty samatha subjects. Visitors are asked to contact the hermitage before arriving. Dāna-based.
