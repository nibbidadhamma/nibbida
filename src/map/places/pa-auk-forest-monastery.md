The main monastery of the Pa-Auk tradition, founded in 1926 about 15 km southeast of Mawlamyine. It teaches samatha and vipassanā, and has a large resident community of monks, nuns and lay practitioners, including foreigners. Applications are made through the monastery's website. Dāna-based.
