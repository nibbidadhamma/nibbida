A Buddhist meditation center at about 1,250 m, with views of Lake Thun and the Bernese Alps. It offers retreats from three days to four weeks, led by various teachers, with emphasis on vipassanā and the heart practices of mettā and karuṇā, and some Tibetan practices. Fee charged.
