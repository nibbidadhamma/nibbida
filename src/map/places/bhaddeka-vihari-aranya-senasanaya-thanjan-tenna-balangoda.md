Also called Thanjan Tenna. A group of forest hermitages that began in 2003, when Ven. Wagadapola Wimalagnana built a hut in the forest near the village, about 23 km southeast of Balangoda. It offers Dhamma talks in Sinhala and English, and the hermitages are open to monks, Sri Lankan and foreign, who wish to stay. Dāna-based.
