A forest monastery of the Sri Kalyani Yogasrama Samstha, in the Galduwa tradition, covering more than 5,000 acres in the Dummiya mountain range, with about 150 monks. The site offers meditation instruction, Dhamma sermons and an application procedure for foreign meditators. Dāna-based.
