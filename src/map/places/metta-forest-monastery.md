A monastery in the Thai Forest tradition, founded in 1990 by Ajaan Suwat Suvaco and directed by Thanissaro Bhikkhu. Its main purpose is to let men ordain as monks, and laypeople are welcome for day visits or overnight stays. Dāna-based.
