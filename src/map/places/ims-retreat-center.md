The retreat center of the Insight Meditation Society, founded in 1975 and rooted in the Theravāda tradition. It offers residential insight (vipassanā) and loving-kindness (mettā) retreats through the year. The Forest Refuge on the same campus is for longer stays. Fee charged, with teachers supported by voluntary dāna.
