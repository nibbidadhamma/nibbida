The English-language retreat center of Wat Suan Mokkh, founded in 1986 in the tradition of Buddhadāsa Bhikkhu. It holds silent 10-day retreats each month, teaching mindfulness with breathing (ānāpānasati). Fee charged, 2,000 baht for everything.
